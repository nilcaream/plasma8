Beat

A beat clock that runs on by itself, timed to the sound as it reaches the microphone. It starts when 3 readings in a row agree, and then never stops: through a breakdown or silence it keeps the last tempo and beat. A reading within 1 BPM of its tempo is the same tempo: after every 4 new hits on the grid the clock takes that reading's beat, and its tempo when the reading is precise. A reading further away is a new tempo, taken only when it is sure: precise readings (to 0.1 BPM) that agree with each other for 2 s in a row, or for 6 s when the beat behind them is weak, as in a stretch with no beat.
