Random walk graphs

One free parameter. On every hit of one beat voice it picks up or down at random and goes one step that way, as far as the intensity allows (none at 0); it holds its place, travels in a short smooth stroke, and arrives exactly on the hit. A step that would leave the range goes the other way instead, so every step is a whole step; each is a little longer, up to a tenth, at random.
